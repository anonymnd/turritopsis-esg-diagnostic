## Observed Turritopsis Design Signals

From the live site at https://turritopsis.org/:

- Visual identity feels institutional, educational, and association-led rather than SaaS-heavy.
- Navigation is content-rich: Accueil, Turritopsis, governance, strategic plan, contacts, registration, events, publications, blog.
- Typography uses Quicksand-like rounded headings.
- Headings are large, friendly, and dark blue/navy.
- The site uses white page sections, light gray dividers, and formal content blocks.
- Accent colors include sky blue and burgundy.
- The brand should feel like an institute or Act Tank, not a startup dashboard.

## Recommended UI Direction

Use a multi-page institutional application with:

- White/light-gray surfaces.
- Burgundy for primary actions and alerts.
- Sky blue for progress, evidence quality, and analytical highlights.
- Quicksand-style headings and clean sans-serif body text.
- Cards with small border radius, not overly rounded SaaS cards.
- Clear page navigation instead of a single landing-page flow.
- Strong distinction between SME workflow and reviewer workflow.

## Template Options

### Option 1: Institutional Portal

Best for the public/product entry point. It explains the ESG platform, shows a current dossier score, and routes users into diagnosis, proofs, AI review, and reports.

### Option 2: Analyst Workspace

Best for Turritopsis/AlifCrowd reviewers. It emphasizes dossier review, evidence checks, AI verdict, and human validation.

### Option 3: Guided SME Wizard

Best for SMEs. It focuses on one question at a time, simple scoring, proof entry, and progress toward final analysis.

## Recommendation

Use all three as different areas of the app:

- Public landing/dashboard: Option 1.
- SME questionnaire: Option 3.
- Reviewer/admin workspace: Option 2.
